# 1.4 — Objectives, Implemented Artifacts and Scope

The project seeks a technical response to the reporting problem, rather than a replacement of the organization's existing Power BI report. It takes FOCUS cost-and-usage files as inputs and develops a reproducible path from those files to documented indicators and a demonstrator dashboard. The intended benefits are clearer responsibility for data and calculations, repeatable processing, easier diagnosis of failures and analytical views prepared for FinOps questions.

The implementation has three linked parts. An independent generator produces fictitious cost-and-usage history from anonymized reference data. A local DuckDB and Streamlit proof of concept tests an end-to-end analytical path. A Databricks implementation on GCP separates DEV and PROD processing, applies a versioned data contract, records operational evidence and prepares Silver, Gold and datamart tables for reporting. Its monthly backfill and incremental Daily workflows address different arrival patterns. The cloud dashboard is a project demonstrator designed to read these products; it is not evidence that Technip Energies has replaced or adopted the current Power BI Cloud Showback.

The work does not use the organization's detailed billing history as a public research dataset. Synthetic inputs permit reproducible engineering tests, but cannot establish actual savings, allocation accuracy for the company's real applications or the experience of its users. Chapters 3 to 5 explain the method, design and implementation; Chapters 6 to 8 examine the available analytical, technical and cost evidence.
