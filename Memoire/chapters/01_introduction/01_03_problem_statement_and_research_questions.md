# 1.3 — Problem Statement and Research Question

The difficulties described in Section 1.2 point to one research problem. Cloud-cost reporting depends on a chain of data preparation, quality checks, business definitions and access decisions. When these responsibilities are separated and FinOps has limited ability to inspect or adapt the chain, new reporting needs can require repeated handoffs. A dedicated data platform may make this work more explicit and reusable, but its value must be assessed against what the project actually builds and verifies.

The research question is:

> How can a governed FinOps data platform transform FOCUS cost-and-usage data into reliable analytical products for cloud showback, and what operational added value does the resulting system demonstrate?

Here, *added value* has a specific meaning. It concerns the ability to process recurring data automatically, detect and trace data-quality failures, reuse documented measures, prepare datamarts for defined questions and support reporting for authorized audiences. It also concerns the potential to reduce the number of handoffs needed for routine changes. Fewer handoffs do not imply that specialist review, business approval or security controls should disappear. A shorter change time and a lower staffing cost would require a measured comparison with the current process; neither is assumed as a result.

The thesis examines this one question through three connected aspects: the reliability of the data path, the usefulness and adaptability of the analytical products, and the governance of access and operation. These are analytical lenses, not three separate research problems. Section 1.5 distinguishes what the implementation can establish from benefits that would require further measurement.
