# 1.5 — Summary of Requirements

The project framing groups ten acceptance requirements under platform reliability, governed self-service and FinOps decision support. Reliability requires identifiable historical and incremental batches, a versioned FOCUS contract, traceable Bronze-to-Gold transformations, reconciliation and repeatable execution. The data products must record their definitions, owner, freshness and quality before they are presented as certified within the project.

Governed self-service requires more than a working dashboard. The proposed acceptance scenario asks a manager to answer cost questions for an authorized domain and an analyst to produce an additional aggregation from a certified datamart. Access tests must cover the dashboard, exports and direct queries; filtering one interface does not secure another. These scenarios remain acceptance criteria until representative identities and permissions have been tested.

Decision support covers showback, period comparisons, documented cost formulas, pipeline status and recovery. The requirements distinguish a calculated price difference from a verified saving and require unknown allocation to remain visible. Chapter 7 reports what the prototype establishes against these criteria. The complete requirement matrix and its proposed evidence are retained in the project framing document.
