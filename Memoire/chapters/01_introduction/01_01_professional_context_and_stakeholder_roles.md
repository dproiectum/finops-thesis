# 1.1 — Professional Context and Stakeholder Roles

This project examines the production and use of cloud-cost information within the host organization's FinOps reporting process. The project framing describes a central Data Platform function, a Power BI team and a separate FinOps team. FinOps defines the financial questions and reviews the resulting indicators; the platform and reporting functions provide the technical means to prepare and present them. These responsibilities explain why a change to a cost measure can involve several teams even when the underlying data is already available.

The existing Cloud Showback dashboard serves FinOps, its management and managers in other domains, according to the student's account. The issue examined here is therefore the reliability and adaptability of the information supplied through that process, together with the degree of analytical autonomy available to authorized users. Section 1.2 describes the reported change workflow.
