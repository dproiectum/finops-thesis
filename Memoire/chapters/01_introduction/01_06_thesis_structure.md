# 1.6 — Thesis Structure

Chapter 2 defines the FinOps concepts and cost measures needed to interpret cloud showback. Chapter 3 explains how the intended outcomes led to the dataset, quality controls, analytical preparation and staged implementation. Chapter 4 presents the architecture and data model; Chapter 5 examines the generator, local proof of concept and Databricks implementation.

Chapter 6 analyzes the resulting cost and allocation measures. Chapter 7 evaluates the technical and reporting evidence, including what remains untested in the existing Power BI and cloud environments. Chapter 8 examines the platform's own build and operating costs. Chapter 9 discusses the findings and the possible effect of closer Data Engineering support within FinOps. Chapter 10 answers the research question within the limits of the evidence and identifies further work.
