# 2.4 — Allocation, Showback and Governed Self-Service

## Allocation and accountability

Allocation associates technology expenditure with an accountable business perimeter such as a domain, application, project, team or cost center. Unallocated costs must remain visible rather than being silently removed because they are themselves a governance signal.

## Showback and chargeback

Showback exposes the cost attributed to a consumer without necessarily performing an internal financial transfer. Chargeback goes further by assigning or recovering that cost through an internal accounting mechanism. The current PFE implements a showback-oriented decision-support scenario; it does not implement an enterprise chargeback process.

## Governed self-service

The proposed access model gives managers information for their authorized perimeter, FinOps consolidated visibility and definition ownership, and authorized analysts read access to certified datamarts. Dashboard-level filtering requires corresponding controls on direct data access. The project has not yet validated those controls with separate user identities.

## Connection to the professional problem

The project framing identifies repeated requests and manual extracts as possible sources of effort. Their frequency and cost have not been measured. A self-service scenario can test whether users complete specified analyses without a new FinOps extract, but an organizational benefit would require evidence from the actual workflow.
