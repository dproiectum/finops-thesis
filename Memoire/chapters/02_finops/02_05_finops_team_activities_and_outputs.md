# 2.5 — FinOps Team Activities and Outputs

The preceding sections define the FinOps concepts used in this study. An internal presentation groups the team's work into four areas and identifies Azure, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), on-premises infrastructure and AI within its scope (Technip Energies, 2026a). Their levels of development differ. The activities below describe the team's remit; they do not measure how often each task is performed.

## Measurement and accountability

- **Cost analysis and reporting.** For a requested period and perimeter, FinOps extracts and checks cost data, identifies the main drivers, and prepares a scoped analysis or dashboard view.
- **Allocation and showback.** The team examines costs by dimensions such as application, domain and tags. It reviews indicators and changes in expenditure so that stakeholders can understand their attributed costs.

## Planning and forecasting

- **Cost exploration.** Before an architecture is fixed, FinOps compares possible services, providers, regions and usage assumptions. The resulting estimate is indicative.
- **Project estimation and follow-up.** Once the architecture is defined, the team estimates costs by resource and environment for review and approval. The documented process includes comparing actual costs with the estimate after deployment.
- **Budget monitoring.** Forecasts and variance analysis help the team discuss changes in expected expenditure with project and budget owners.

## Cost optimization

- **Resource and architecture review.** The team examines cleanup, rightsizing and design changes as possible ways to reduce unnecessary expenditure.
- **Commitments and savings follow-up.** It reviews reservations and Savings Plans, identifies unused commitments, and tracks the effects of optimization actions.

## Governance and operations

- **Coordination and controls.** FinOps participates in cost reviews, contract discussions, guardrails and action tracking with other teams.
- **Extending cost visibility.** On-premises data-centre onboarding is in progress. The presentation identifies AI cost projections and broader data-quality automation as work still under development.

This PFE addresses a narrower part of the team's work. Its platform prepares and checks synthetic, Azure-derived cost-and-usage data for analytical tables and showback. It does not approve project estimates, purchase commitments, implement accounting chargeback, or complete the team's forecasting, on-premises and AI processes.

Source for this section: Technip Energies, *FinOps Corner*, 29 September 2026. Confidential internal presentation; no public URL.
