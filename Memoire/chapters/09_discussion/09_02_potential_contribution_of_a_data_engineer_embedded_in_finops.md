# 9.2 — Potential Contribution of a Data Engineer Embedded in FinOps

## 9.2.1 From observation to hypothesis

The workflow in section 1.2 separates management feedback, relay by the manager and implementation by an external contributor. The issue extends beyond dashboard access to FinOps' ability to change data, indicators and reporting directly.

A Data Engineer close to the FinOps business team could bring requirement interpretation nearer to implementation. This proposal does not remove supervisor approval or automatically replace the Data Platform and Power BI teams.

## 9.2.2 Capabilities and potential benefits to assess

| Dimension | Potential contribution | Required evidence or condition |
|---|---|---|
| Responsiveness | More direct translation of business feedback into a testable change | Compare end-to-end delay, waiting and iterations |
| Data handling | Adapt pipelines, contracts, datamarts and aggregates within FinOps | Authorized skills, permissions and technical scope |
| Business consistency | Validate cost definitions and investigate inconsistent KPIs with FinOps | Documented business reviews, tests and reconciliation |
| Knowledge retention | Retain context, decisions and knowledge within the team | Documentation, version control, code review and continuity |
| Human cost | Possible reduction in coordination time or some external work | Total-cost comparison at equivalent scope and quality, including the embedded role |

Changing visuals, DAX measures or Power BI models may require specialist BI skills. The Data Engineer title alone does not guarantee that capability; responsibilities with the Power BI specialist must be explicit.

## 9.2.3 Conditions and limits

The target model could retain a shared platform and standards, an embedded or dedicated FinOps Data Engineer for data products, and Power BI collaboration for reporting. Greater business proximity is not evidence of a Data Mesh implementation.

Changes remain subject to access controls, tests, business acceptance and publication rules. Peer review and written handover reduce dependence on one person.

An embedded role adds cost and may not justify a full-time position given the request volume. Compare a part-time dedicated resource and a mixed model as well. External delivery may remain valuable for specialized or occasional needs.

## 9.2.4 Proposed evaluation

For a small set of representative authorized requests, record request, approval, handoff, work start, delivery and acceptance dates, plus active time by role and iteration count. Separate elapsed delay from human effort.

Compare observations with a clearly defined embedded scenario, or with a measured pilot if one exists. A simulation does not establish actual company improvement. Chapter 8 will value effort, external services and recurring costs without assuming rates or savings.
