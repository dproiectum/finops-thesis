# 3.6 — FinOps Data Product Delivery and Operation

The dashboard is treated as a stakeholder-facing analytical data product with defined questions, consumers, measures and maintenance expectations. This use of the term follows Google Cloud's definition, which includes dashboards (Google Cloud, n.d.). Datamarts are reusable analytical products; the platform provides their production, validation and distribution.

Delivery requires an identifiable deployed version, verified permissions and visibility of the last successful update, covered periods and known quality limitations. Issues must be traceable to inputs, rules and runs. The PFE's production environment is not equated with enterprise deployment.

Acceptance combines calculation and access checks with defined user tasks, such as identifying a cost driver or examining allocation. Results are compared with reference outputs and representative feedback where available. Technical success alone does not demonstrate stakeholder adoption, reduced effort or realized savings; those claims require separate evidence.
